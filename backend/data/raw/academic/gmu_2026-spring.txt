Academic Calendar for LLM Courses
For Spring 2026, LLM courses adhere to the U.S. campus calendar rather than the Mason Korea academic calendar. Most LLM courses align with 8 Week: Session I (S1: 1/12/2026 - 3/9/2026) and 8 Week: Session II (S2: 3/16/2026 - 5/10/2026) schedules. Session I courses adhere to S1 liability dates, and Session II courses adhere to S2 liability dates.
In some cases, there can be full-term courses, which adhere to Law School full-term academic calendar liability dates. However, individual course dates may vary from the general term dates. Be sure to verify specific course dates on Patriot Web.
For term schedule and liability dates for Session I (S1: 1/12/2026 - 3/9/2026) and Session II (S2: 3/16/2026 - 5/10/2026) courses, please visit the Registrar’s Office website:
Spring 2026 Academic Calendar
(Go to “Part of Term and Tuition Liability Dates”)
For term schedule and liability dates for Law School Full-Term courses, please visit Antonin Scalia Law School’s academic calendar page:
Spring 2026 Law School Academic Calendar
* All dates are subject to change.
