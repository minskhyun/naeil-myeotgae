ACADEMIC CALENDAR 2026-2027
The academic year 2026-2027 opens on August 24, 2026 and closes on August 28, 2027.
The academic year consists of 2 semesters:
→ Courses for the 1st semester will be completed at the end of November, followed by an exam period in December.
→ Courses for the 2nd semester start at the end of February, followed by an exam period in June.
Yearlong courses cover 2 semesters. For Ba1 yearlong courses, a partial examination can be taken in December, 2026.
Resit exams for yearlong courses fall in August, 2027 only.
Resit exams are organized for the first-term courses in February, 2027 and for the second-term courses in August, 2027.
Catch-up weeks are for revision classes or additional exercises.

majeure;
